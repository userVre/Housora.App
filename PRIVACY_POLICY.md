## 3. Personal data we collect

### 3.1 Data you provide

- **Account and profile data:** name, email address, profile image, authentication identifiers, studio or organization name, role, preferences, language, currency, measurement system, and team membership.
- **Customer content:** room, property, furniture, garden, exterior, or other images; prompts; project names; notes; selected objects; selection masks; design preferences; generated images; 3D models; AR-ready assets; saved references; and files or links you submit.
- **Collaboration data:** invitations, project permissions, comments, approvals, and the identity of collaborators or clients you invite.
- **Support and communications:** messages, feedback, survey responses, bug reports, and attachments you send to us.
- **Billing data:** plan, credit purchases and usage, invoice and transaction identifiers, billing country, tax status, payment status, refunds, and fraud-risk signals. Payment-card details are handled by our payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Housora.
- **Legal and consent records:** acceptance of terms, privacy and cookie choices, age or authority confirmations, and privacy-rights requests.

Do not upload government identifiers, financial account credentials, medical records, biometric templates, intimate imagery, or other sensitive personal data unless Housora expressly requests and supports that use.

### 3.2 Data collected automatically

- **Device and connection data:** IP address, approximate country or region derived from IP, browser and device type, operating system, language, time zone, referring URL, and technical identifiers.
- **Usage and diagnostic data:** pages and screens viewed, buttons and features used, project and generation workflow milestones, timestamps, duration, errors, latency, API outcome, credit consumption, and crash or performance information.
- **Cookie and local-storage data:** authentication, security, preferences, consent state, analytics identifiers, and session continuity.
- **Optional session replay data:** interactions such as clicks, scrolling, navigation, viewport size, and rendered interface state, only where enabled in accordance with applicable consent rules. Housora intends to mask text inputs and exclude uploaded and generated visual content from replay. Session replay must not intentionally record passwords, payment fields, prompts, private project notes, uploaded images, generated images, masks, or 3D assets.

### 3.3 Data from other sources

We may receive account data from Clerk, payment and subscription status from Whop or another payment provider, processing results from AI and 3D providers, invitation data from other Housora users, and fraud, security, or compliance information from service providers and public sources where permitted by law.
